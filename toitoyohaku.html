iPhone・iPad向け／公開準備中。無料・広告なし・アプリ内課金なし。

小さな問いと、あなたの余白

日々の小さな問いをきっかけに、浮かんだ言葉や手書きを残すノートです。正解も、長い文章も必要ありません。書かずに次へ進んでも大丈夫です。

操作表示と問いは日本語です。記録は端末内に保存され、アプリ独自のクラウド同期や端末間共有はありません。

使い方とサポート

  1. 問いを読み、「文字入力」または「手書き」で記録します。
  2. 「端末に保存済み」の表示を確認します。保存エラーの場合は画面の案内を確認し、端末の空き容量を確保して再度保存してください。
  3. 「次の問い」で進み、「読み返す」でこれまでの記録を確認できます。

アプリを削除すると記録が失われる場合があります。機種変更時の自動引き継ぎは保証していません。大切な記録はスクリーンショットなどで別途保管してください。

Toi to Yohaku — Questions and Space

A quiet notebook for responding to small Japanese prompts with words or handwriting. For iPhone and iPad. Preparing for release. Free, with no ads or in-app purchases. The app interface and prompts are in Japanese.

Support

Choose text or handwriting, check the saved indicator, then move to the next prompt. Use the history button to revisit entries. Records are stored on your device. There is no app-provided cloud sync or device sharing. Deleting the app may delete its records; transfer to a new device is not guaranteed. Keep a separate copy of important notes, for example as screenshots.

Contact elegant97_lay@icloud.com with your device, OS/app version and steps to reproduce an issue. Do not send private journal content or passwords.

Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Operated by Mori Lab. The app stores text and drawings locally and does not send them to the developer. No analytics, advertising, tracking or account registration is included. OS backups, if enabled, follow your settings and Apple's services; the app has no independent cloud sync.

If you contact support, your email address and message are used only as needed to respond and investigate. Information is deleted when no longer needed; you can request earlier deletion at the same address. It is not shared with third parties except as required by law.
